About the page

Ghazal Rekhta is an archive of the whole Urdu poetry tradition: classical ghazals, Sufi verse and qawwali, and the modern poets who carry it forward. Through short, aesthetic reels it moves across centuries, from the classical masters of the ghazal to the Sufi poets and the qawwals who sang their words, to the voices still writing today. The names are countless, and every one of them has a place here.

The focus is simple: rich classical verse, told through visual storytelling that stays with you. Candlelit edits, old black-and-white footage, and lyric breakdowns that open up what a ghazal is really saying.
